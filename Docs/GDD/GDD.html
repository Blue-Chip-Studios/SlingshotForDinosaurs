1High concept & fantasy

“Pull back. Let go. Bounce. Catch a Colossal Cosmic T-Rex.”

Developer Requested A distance-launch simulator. You train slingshot power, fire bait down one long prehistoric canyon of biomes, catch the dinosaur it summons, and build a park enclosure that earns cash online and offline.

Player fantasy: the electric moment your bait rockets past five biomes on a lucky mid-air bounce, slams into the glowing volcano, and summons a 1-in-1,000 Cosmic T-Rex that towers over everyone else's park.

Genre: incremental simulator + collection (distance-launch loop with idle income, and mutation/size flex for social status).

2Audience & platforms

Audience Dev
Ages 7-15. Short attention, high appetite for spectacle and rarity. Reading kept to big words and icons.
Platforms Dev
Mobile & tablet first. Single-thumb hold-to-power, giant tap-anywhere bounce. Full PC and console parity.

3Design pillars

⚡ Dopamine in 20 seconds

First launch and first dinosaur within 20 s of spawning, and cash ticks up immediately. Dev

👀 See where you're going

Every biome is visible straight ahead from the rim. Distant zones glow, so the goal is physical, not a menu. Dev

🎲 Every catch is a lottery

Size × mutation rolls make each catch a reveal. Rare ones are flexed visibly in your park. Dev

👍 One thumb, zero tiny menus

Big buttons, physical pedestals in the world, and almost no screen-based management. Dev

4Core loop

Developer Requested Train → Launch with a timed bounce → Catch (size and mutation roll) → Place in the enclosure (cash online and offline) → Upgrade to reach deeper biomes.

BCDK Recommended Each upgrade track has one job. Slingshots = distance, Bait = luck, Power = distance (grind), Enclosure = earning capacity. Players always know why they're buying, and the shop needs no explanation.

8Mechanics

Launch

Distance (m) = Power × Slingshot multiplier × Charge (0.5-1.0) × (1 + Bounce bonus) × event/admin multipliers. Charge fills over 1.2 s and holding past full gives no penalty. BCDK Kids should never lose a launch for over-holding.

Mid-air bounce Dev

Each flight passes 1-3 geysers or ledges along its arc, computed by the server from the launch seed. Tapping inside a window bounces the bait: Perfect +35% (±0.12 s), Good +20% (±0.25 s), OK +10% (±0.4 s). Each bounce also adds +10% luck to the catch roll. A missed window means no penalty. Slow-mo (0.4×) during windows on mobile.

Follow-cam Dev

Spring-damped camera behind and above the bait, widening FOV with speed. A short impact shake and zoom-in on the reveal, then a fast fly-back to the player.

Catch

The landing biome sets the species pool. The server rolls species (bait luck), then size, then mutation. A reveal card appears, the dino model drops in with VFX, and then it walks to your plot. Full enclosure → "Replace weakest / Sell for 60 s of income" popup, with an Auto-replace weaker toggle. BCDK No inventory management, and no catch is ever wasted.

Training

Stand on a pad: +Power every 1 s × pad multiplier. Tapping TRAIN adds up to 5 extra trains/s (server rate-limited). BCDK Passive gain is mobile-friendly; tapping gives active players an edge.

11World: the Canyon

Developer Requested One continuous valley stretching straight ahead from the launch rim. All five biomes are visible, and the far ones glow.

BCDK Recommended Displayed meters are decoupled from world size. Each biome is a 400-stud segment, and meters map piecewise and logarithmically into it, so the whole canyon is about 2,100 studs. Simulator numbers must grow exponentially, but the world must fit on a low-end phone and stay fully visible from the rim.

  • Rim: 8 enclosure plots in an arc facing the canyon, the central launch platform, the training station, and the slingshot and bait pedestals.
  • Jungle: fat-canopy trees, ferns, bright greens. Swamp: mangroves, lily pads, mist. Tar Pits: bubbling black pools, bones, amber rocks.
  • Ice Age: glaciers, frozen dinos in ice. Volcano: a towering cone with a lava glow visible from the rim, and an eruption on Meteor Storms.
  • Geysers and ledges (bounce points) line the canyon floor, and biome gates have floating distance signs.

13Weather events & Admin panel

Developer Requested A recurring 20-minute world cycle: 15 min clear + 5 min event, alternating:

☄️ Meteor Storm
Dark red sky, falling meteors with impact craters, the volcano erupts. Mutation odds ×3, and Magma and Cosmic ×5.
❄️ Ice Age
Snow, frost fog, cyan sky. Frost odds ×6 and size odds ×2.

A countdown widget on the HUD (bottom-right) shows the next event. Dev A Server-Wide Meteor Storm dev product (149 R$) starts or extends a 5-minute storm and credits the buyer server-wide.

Admin Abuse panel Dev

Developer-only (UserId allowlist plus group-rank check on the server). Trigger Meteor Storm or Ice Age, set server luck ×2/×5/×10 for N minutes, force the next launch for everyone to roll a rare spawn, and broadcast an announcement. Every admin action is logged to analytics.

14Social, multiplayer & servers

  • Dev 8-player servers for locked 60 FPS on low-end mobile.
  • Every plot is visible from the rim, so rare dinos are flexed by being there. A server announcement plays for Huge+, any mutation above Golden, and every Legendary.
  • BCDK Leaderboard columns Best Distance and $/s. Status is the main motivator for this audience.
  • Other players' launches render as light client-side arcs. Only your own launch gets the follow-cam.

17UI/UX

Built from the supplied references (Docs/UI/References/). The full spec is in Docs/UI/DesignSystem.md.

  • Shape language: chunky rounded rectangles (12-16 px radius), 3-4 px dark outline, 4 px drop shadow "lip" under buttons.
  • Type: Fredoka One for display (white, dark stroke) and Fredoka/Gotham for body. Big numbers everywhere.
  • Colour coding: Shop = green, Index = blue, Pass = gold, red = close/unaffordable, orange = affordable, purple = premium/Robux.
  • HUD: left button stack (Shop, Index, Passes), bottom-left Cash and Power, bottom-right event timer, right column of pass/offer icons, top-centre biome/distance banner. The centre of the screen is kept clear for launching.
  • Moments: "NEW!" catch reveal card (sticker, size and mutation chips, $/s), Welcome Back popup, enclosure-full choice popup, event banner.
  • Safe-area aware, minimum 64 px touch targets, controller focus rings, UIScale per device.

18Art direction: Stylized

Developer Requested Bright, chunky, high-saturation stylized prehistoric world with crisp silhouettes, neon mutation auras and dramatic impact particles.

Jungle #2FB344 Swamp #6B8E23 Tar #3A2F2A Ice #5EC8F2 Lava #FF6A1A Outline #1E1B2E
  • Dinos: chunky stylised proportions (big heads, stubby limbs, readable silhouettes), 1.5-3k triangles each, one shared 512 px gradient atlas, and mutations as colour and material swaps plus aura particles.
  • World: Blender modular kit per biome (rocks, trees, props, cliff walls) with soft bevels and exaggerated shapes. Distant biomes use low-poly silhouettes and emissive accents so they glow from the rim.
  • Lighting: Future, warm sun, soft shadows, light atmosphere haze, gentle saturation and bloom. Each event gets its own Lighting/Atmosphere preset.
  • VFX: star bursts, soft puffs, swooshes, ground-impact rings and dust. Bait trail colour by bait tier.

19Audio direction

Playful, punchy and prehistoric: rubber creak and twang, whooshes, bouncy geyser "boing", deep roar on reveal (pitch by size), a sparkle layer by mutation, coin ticks, and a short jungle-drum music loop with event stingers. Generated with ElevenLabs where available, with Toolbox fallbacks documented.

Launch flow (server-authoritative): client RequestLaunch(charge, aim) → server validates the cooldown and computes base meters, a seed and bounce windows → client animates the deterministic arc → client Bounce(t) → server checks t against the windows (±tolerance, server clock) → server finalises meters → rolls species, size and mutation → saves → CatchResult to the client → reveal.

Data: ProfileConfig schema v1 with Version and migration hook. Keys: Cash, Power, Slingshot, Bait, Slots, Dinos[] {Id, Species, Size, Mutation, CaughtAt}, Index{}, BestDistance, LastOnline, FTUEStep, Passes cache, PurchaseIds{}, Settings{AutoReplace}.

22Performance & platforms

  • 8-player servers. StreamingEnabled with the canyon's far silhouettes as persistent low-poly models.
  • Dinos are visual-only client models: the server holds data only, and clients build and pool dino models from the shared replica. Animation is client-side and only updates within 250 studs.
  • Launches replicate intent plus seed; other clients reconstruct arcs locally. No physics bodies for bait.
  • Income is computed server-side once per second per player and replicated as a single value; the cash-tick visuals are client-side.
  • Budgets: under 120k visible triangles on mobile, a shared texture atlas, and under 40 active particle emitters.

23Content pipeline

Blender MCP (stylised dinos, biome kits, slingshots, pedestals) → FBX export to Assets/Exports → Open Cloud upload with the .env key (upload script only) → asset IDs into Config. ElevenLabs for SFX and music. UI art as images with 9-slice.

24Live-ops

Saturday "Admin Abuse" live events (as the brief describes) using the Admin panel. New biome drops (Sky Islands, Deep Ocean), limited exclusive dinos on rotation, event mutations (e.g. Halloween "Spooky"), and server-side config overrides for prices and odds.

28Decisions (BCDK Recommended)

  • Displayed distance decoupled from world size (piecewise log mapping, 400 studs per biome).
  • Server-authoritative launch, bounce windows and catch rolls.
  • Auto-train on pads plus tap-to-boost.
  • Offline income at 50%, capped at 8 h (12 h VIP).
  • Enclosure-full choice popup with an auto-replace toggle.
  • Exclusive Dino: one per player, based on the best other dino.
  • Robux skips only one or two tiers ahead. Odds disclosed. No dark patterns.
  • Guaranteed Perfect first bounce and Big first catch.
  • Added: Dino Index, leaderboard, Starter Pack, cash packs scaled to income.
